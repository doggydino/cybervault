Common Instructor Discussion Points

Ask students:

Why do two identical passwords produce different hashes?

Answer:

bcrypt generates a random salt every time.


-----

Ask:

Can we "decrypt" the stored password?

Answer:

No. Hashes are one-way functions. During login, we hash the entered password and compare it against the stored hash using bcrypt.checkpw()


--------

Ask:

Why don't we compare two hashes ourselves?

Answer:

Because bcrypt stores the salt within the hash and performs the comparison securely.
